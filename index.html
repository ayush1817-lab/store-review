How the data would get here

Today, checking a week means logging into Retail Solutions and Timepoint, pulling reports store by store, re-entering last year's dates and comparing by hand. This page does the comparing automatically. In a live version the numbers arrive on their own every Monday morning.

1Retail SolutionsSales ex VAT, gross profit, departments, waste and customers for every store
2TimepointHours worked per person, per store. Employee ID only, no names
3Weekly importStored once, so last year's weeks are always on hand. No date re-entry
4This pageLabour estimate, comparisons and flags worked out automatically

Ways to connect, most to least automatic

1
Data feed from each vendorRetail Solutions and Timepoint provide read-only access or a scheduled weekly export. Nobody touches anything.
2
Read-only access to the RS Head Office serverArranged with whoever maintains the server.
3
Scheduled reports inside RSEmailed or saved to a shared folder each Monday, if RS supports it.
4
Fallback: one export a weekAll stores, one week, a few minutes. Everything else on this page stays automatic.

History (the last 12–18 months) is loaded once at the start. After that, only the new week is added.

What each number means

SalesSales ex VAT from Retail Solutions. Excludes agency sales such as Lotto and phone top-ups.
Gross profit / GP %Sales minus cost of goods, as reported by Retail Solutions. GP % = gross profit ÷ sales.
HoursEvery person's hours worked in Timepoint for that store and week, added up.
Labour cost (estimate)Each person's hours × the rate in Labour rates, plus employer PRSI if switched on (9% on weekly pay up to €552, 11.25% above). Not payroll.
Labour %Estimated labour cost ÷ sales.
Sales per labour hourSales ÷ hours. Uses hours only, so it doesn't depend on the rate estimate.
Left after labourGross profit − estimated labour. Not net profit: rent, energy, insurance and other costs aren't included.
WasteWaste logged in Retail Solutions, at retail value, shown separately. To confirm: whether RS already deducts it from gross profit.
Customers / basketTransactions from Retail Solutions. Average basket = sales ÷ customers.
Last yearThe same Monday–Sunday week 52 weeks earlier, so weekdays line up (Mon 19–Sun 25 Oct 2026 is compared with Mon 20–Sun 26 Oct 2025).

Labour rates

Timepoint gives hours, not pay, so labour cost is estimated from these rates. Every number on the page updates as you type.
